### Proposed process descriptions

These are editorial summaries for approval, not verbatim stage descriptions. The first four derive from Seasia's [consulting service overview](https://www.seasiainfotech.com/artificial-intelligence-consulting); the final stage combines its scale-review label with [AI/ML lifecycle support](https://www.seasiainfotech.com/ai-ml-services).

| Stage | Proposed supporting copy |
| --- | --- |
| Discover | Assess business needs, available data and technical readiness to identify feasible AI opportunities. |
| Strategize | Prioritize use cases and align the roadmap with business goals, success measures and operating requirements. |
| Architect | Define the cloud, model, data and security foundations needed for the selected use cases. |
| Build | Implement a pilot or MVP to test the solution and validate its value before a wider rollout. |
| Scale | Review the pilot and plan broader adoption, ongoing monitoring and model improvement. |

## 3. Original visual system proposed for approval

Use warm paper #F3F0E9, ink #191C1A, muted mineral #D9DED8 and deep forest #245B49. Forest is reserved for primary actions, active states and diagram emphasis. Use occasional ink sections to establish rhythm around the AI system and outcomes.

Typography: a modern grotesk such as Instrument Sans, paired with a restrained monospace for indexes and diagram annotations. Desktop display 88–120 px, section headings 56–76 px, body 18–20 px. Mobile display 44–56 px, section headings 32–40 px, body 18 px. Font loading and licensing will be checked when assets are selected.

A 12-column desktop grid with 64–96 px outer margins becomes four columns with 20–24 px margins on mobile. Use square or lightly softened edges, fine rules, asymmetric spacing and original vector diagrams. Proposed hero motif: an architectural framework whose individual paths converge into an ordered structure, repeated selectively in the AI and capability sections.

Desktop interactions must also work by clicking and keyboard navigation. Mobile converts sticky stories and horizontal sequences to vertical content or accordions. No scroll locking. Respect reduced motion by showing final content immediately and disabling parallax/crossfades; all numbers are available as static text. Focus states, readable contrast and at least 44 px control targets are part of the implementation.
